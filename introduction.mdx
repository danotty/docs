SignalArk turns public market activity into your daily call list. Instead of relying on static lists, SignalArk monitors your market in real time, scores every signal across 5 transparent dimensions, and tells you exactly **why** you should reach out to an account **now**.

## The Detect → Stack → Route Framework

Our platform operates on a simple, powerful framework:

1. **Detect**: We monitor 15+ sources (job boards, news, social media, keyword tracking, and tech stacks) to catch buying signals as they happen.
2. **Stack**: Signals aren't treated in isolation. We unify them into a single account timeline, calculating source diversity and compounding signal scores.
3. **Route**: Using Play Templates and GTME Plays, we route actionable intelligence directly to your CRM, Slack, or webhooks.
